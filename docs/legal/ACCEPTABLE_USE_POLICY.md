# Blaze Break — Acceptable Use Policy (DRAFT)

**Status: DRAFT — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED before publication** (mainly for
UK-law phrasing/enforceability review; the substance below is grounded
in this product's real abuse-control mechanisms, not invented).

*Version: 0.1 (draft) · Not yet published · UK English*

By using Blaze Break, you agree not to:

1. **Break the law** — use the service for any illegal purpose, or in a
   way that infringes anyone else's rights.
2. **Harass or abuse others** — including anyone reached through the
   Guardian safety feature or, for organisation accounts, colleagues
   using the platform.
3. **Attack the service** — attempt to disrupt, overload, or gain
   unauthorised access to Blaze Break's systems, including our Firebase/
   Google Cloud infrastructure.
4. **Bypass access restrictions** — attempt to circumvent subscription
   tier limits, rate limits, or capability quotas (for example, scripted
   or automated calls designed to exceed your tier's fair-use allowance
   for Nova chat, Nova Live voice, or SMS features).
5. **Scrape or automate abusively** — use bots, scrapers, or other
   automated means to extract data from the service beyond your own
   normal, individual use.
6. **Reverse engineer**, except to the extent this is expressly permitted
   by law and cannot be excluded by contract — **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED**
   for the precise UK-compliant wording here.
7. **Share your account** — your account is for your own individual use;
   credential sharing undermines both security and the personalisation
   the product is built around.
8. **Abuse messaging/notification features** — misuse the Guardian SMS
   alert, ally nudge, or email features to send unwanted, harassing, or
   excessive messages to any recipient, including yourself in a way
   designed to exhaust the fair-use caps that exist to protect the
   service and other users.
9. **Upload unsafe or malicious content** — including malware, or content
   that violates a third party's rights.
10. **Attempt to access another user's data**, including attempting to
    exploit a bug or misconfiguration to view another account's private
    wellbeing content, Nova conversations, or organisation data.
11. **Interfere with the safety mechanisms** built into the product —
    for example, attempting to trigger Guardian alerts to a contact
    without the alerting user's own genuine, explicit action, or
    attempting to make Nova bypass its safety instructions.
12. **Commit fraud** — including payment/subscription manipulation once
    live billing exists (for example, attempting to fraudulently dispute
    a legitimate charge, or manipulate entitlement records).

## Enforcement

We may investigate suspected violations and take proportionate action,
which can include a warning, temporary suspension, or termination of
your account, consistent with `TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md` §11.

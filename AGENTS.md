# Blaze Break: Anti-Chaos Rules

## Product Statement
Blaze Break is a burnout recovery operating system that helps high achievers understand their burnout pattern, rebuild their energy, practise boundaries, recover properly, and prevent relapse — guided by Nova, their AI recovery coach.

## Core Pillars
1. **Understand**: Burnout diagnostic and Fingerprint.
2. **Rebuild**: Energy Budget.
3. **Practice**: Boundary Rehearsal and Workload Negotiator.
4. **Track**: Recovery Debt Inventory.
5. **Learn**: Chapter-to-Action Engine (Reflect).
6. **Support**: Guardian Protocol (Support Circle).
7. **Scale**: Organization Pulse Dashboard.

## Anti-Chaos Rules
- **Do not invent extra features.** Stick to the roadmap.
- **Do not invent extra burnout types.** Use the established 5 archetypes.
- **Do not turn Nova into a therapist.** Nova is an AI recovery coach for high achievers focus on ambition and stability.
- **Do not make medical claims.** This is not a medical device.
- **Do not create messy navigation.** Keep the sidebar clean and the top bar consistent.
- **Do not build all features at once.** Iterative development only.
- **Start with fake data before connecting a backend.** (Currently using localStorage and mock data as requested).
- **Use simple scoring before AI.** Simple point rewards for engagement.
- **Every feature must connect to one of the 7 main pillars.**
- **Every page must give the user one clear next step.**

# Nova and AI at Blaze Break — what to know

*Version: 0.1 (draft) · Not yet published · UK English*

## Nova is AI, not a person

Nova is an AI coach. She isn't a human, and she isn't a therapist,
counsellor, or doctor. Nova can sometimes get things wrong, misread what
you meant, or give advice that doesn't fit your specific situation.
Trust your own judgement, and treat Nova's suggestions as one useful
input, not the final word on anything important.

## What Nova is for

Nova is here to help with burnout recovery, energy management, and
sustainable performance — practical, everyday support for high
achievers under pressure. She is **not** a substitute for professional
medical, psychiatric, or psychological care, and she doesn't diagnose
anything. Where the product uses a validated screening tool (like the
GAD-7 anxiety check-in), it's presented honestly as a self-report
screening instrument, never as a diagnosis.

## How your information reaches Nova

- What you type or say to Nova during a conversation is sent to our AI
  provider (Google Gemini by default) to generate a response. That
  conversation isn't saved by us as a permanent transcript — it exists
  for the length of your session.
- Some features are different by design: **voice journal entries** and
  **resentment tracker entries** are deliberately saved, because the
  whole point is to look back on them — that's clearly part of using
  those specific tools.
- With your permission, Nova can remember specific things about you —
  preferences, patterns, things you've told her — to make future
  conversations feel less repetitive. You're always in control of this:
  you can see every memory Nova holds, edit it, or delete it, in
  Settings, at any time.
- Full technical detail is in `PRIVACY_NOTICE.md`.

## What Nova will never do without you asking

- **Nova never contacts anyone on your behalf automatically.** If you've
  set up a trusted "Guardian" contact for safety support, Nova can never
  decide on its own to alert them — that only ever happens when you take
  a clear, explicit action to send it yourself.
- Nova doesn't score or label you with a hidden "risk level" that's
  shared with anyone else.
- If your account is sponsored by your employer, Nova's conversations
  with you are never visible to them — see the next section.

## If your account is through your employer

Your employer can see aggregate, anonymised participation trends across
the whole team — never your individual Nova conversations, journal
entries, or anything else tied to you by name. This is built into how
the product actually works, not just something we promise in writing —
see `PRIVACY_NOTICE.md` §12 for the detail.

# Blaze Break — Cancellation & Refund Policy (DRAFT)

**Status: DRAFT — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED before publication.**

*Version: 0.1 (draft) · Not yet published · UK English*

## Current reality, stated honestly

Blaze Break does not yet have a live, self-service payment system. No
Stripe, Apple In-App Purchase, or Google Play Billing integration exists
in the product today (`docs/STRIPE_PRODUCTS.md`,
`docs/MOBILE_SUBSCRIPTIONS.md`). Paid-tier access is currently
provisioned directly by Blaze Break, not purchased through checkout.
**This policy describes how cancellation and refunds will work once each
payment route is live**, so the commercial position is decided and
documented in advance rather than invented at launch under time
pressure.

## Once web/Stripe billing is live

- You can cancel your subscription at any time from your Blaze Break
  account settings.
- Cancelling stops future renewal. You keep access to your paid tier
  until the end of the period you've already paid for, then your account
  reverts to the Free tier.
- **Refund requests**: **COMMERCIAL DECISION REQUIRED** — the business
  needs to decide the refund window/policy (e.g. a short cooling-off
  period, case-by-case discretion, or no refunds outside statutory
  rights) before this is published. UK consumers have certain statutory
  cancellation rights for digital content and services under the
  Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 — **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** to
  confirm how these apply to an ongoing subscription service with
  immediate access on purchase (the 14-day cancellation right can be
  affected by the customer's own acknowledgement that immediate access
  waives part of that right — this needs a solicitor's confirmation of
  the correct wording, not an invented clause here).

## Once Apple App Store billing is live

- Apple subscriptions are managed entirely through your Apple ID
  settings (Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on iOS).
- **We cannot directly issue an Apple refund.** Refund requests for an
  Apple purchase must go through Apple, at
  reportaproblem.apple.com or Apple's own support channels — this is
  Apple's process, not ours, and we will not misrepresent that we can
  action it.
- Cancelling in Apple's settings stops future renewal; access continues
  until the end of the current billing period, per Apple's standard
  subscription behaviour.

## Once Google Play billing is live

- Google Play subscriptions are managed entirely through the Google Play
  Store app or play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.
- **We cannot directly issue a Google Play refund.** Refund requests for
  a Google Play purchase must go through Google Play's own support
  process.
- Cancelling in Google Play's settings stops future renewal; access
  continues until the end of the current billing period.

## What we will never do

We will not display "Refund issued" in the app for a request that has
merely been submitted or is pending review by us or by the relevant
store. A refund is only ever confirmed once it has actually been
processed by the payment provider in question.

## Coaching and course cancellation

If/when 1:1 coaching, executive coaching, or the Signature Course become
separately purchasable products (rather than part of a subscription
tier), their own cancellation terms will be defined here —
**COMMERCIAL DECISION REQUIRED**, not invented in this draft, since
these are higher-touch, potentially scheduled/delivered-in-advance
services where a blanket digital-subscription refund policy may not fit.

## Digital content — statutory position

**LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** — UK consumer law treats "digital content"
(e.g. a downloadable course, a one-off digital product) differently from
an ongoing subscription service in some respects. If Blaze Break ever
sells discrete digital content rather than only subscription access,
this section needs its own, separately reviewed treatment.
